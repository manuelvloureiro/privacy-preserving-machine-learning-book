Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning

Manuel V. Loureiro

Four books and a set of study notes. They are written to be read in order: the course gives the foundations, the full edition the depth, the practitioner edition the practice, the OpenDP book one library end to end, and each study note one question after the book it extends. If you build or review private systems and want results rather than proofs, start at step 3.

Reading order

Start here. Differential privacy from first principles, one topic per chapter, with every proof and little else: why anonymization fails, the definition, its mechanisms and composition, answering many queries, private learning and statistics, lower bounds and deployments.
